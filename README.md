# Olist E-Commerce Analytics

An end-to-end analytics project analyzing revenue performance and operational logistics for a Brazilian e-commerce platform. Built with MySQL for data extraction and Power BI for visualization.

---

## Tools
- MySQL — data extraction and transformation
- Power BI Desktop — dashboard and visualization

---

## Dataset
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset — [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

---

## Business Questions
1. What is the total revenue and order volume by year and month?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
3. Who are the top sellers by revenue?
4. How does average delivery time vary by state?
5. Which states have the most orders and highest revenue?
6. What are the most popular payment methods and average order value by type?
7. What is the overall order cancellation rate?
8. What is the average freight cost by product category?

---

## Key Findings
- São Paulo (SP) dominates revenue at $6M, nearly 3x the second highest state
- Health Beauty and Watches & Gifts are the top revenue categories
- Average delivery time is 12 days nationally, but remote northern states like Roraima (RR) average 29 days
- Credit card users have the highest average order value at $164, compared to $99 for voucher users
- 92% on-time delivery rate across all orders
